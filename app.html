BitByte

Your internet is sold in megabits. Your files are in megabytes. How long will it really take?

About this

Time = size in bits ÷ speed in bits per second. There are 8 bits in a byte, so 100 Mbps moves 12.5 MB per second at most, and 1 Gbps is 125 MB per second (omcalculator). A 50 MB file on 100 Mbps takes 4 seconds before overhead (webcaretakers). Overhead from TCP/IP, TLS and retransmits is typically 3 to 8 percent; 5% is an assumption for an idle line. Shared Wi-Fi, slow servers and speed caps can make the real figure much worse. About
